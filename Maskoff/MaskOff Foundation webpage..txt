 MaskOff Foundation webpage.

This layout departs from the esoteric gold and ivory of ElevateCraft and fully adopts the MaskOff identity system: stark black for truth, sharp white for clarity, and deep red for danger and awareness. The typography uses bold, sharp sans-serif fonts to deliver the raw, direct, and unyielding tone you established.
